# Kisan Bandhu — Idea Notes (SIH submission, Team Victus)

## Problem

- **Colossal bottlenecks** — massive post-harvest vehicle queues and multi-hour wait times choking APMC yards (4–8 hours at the gate).
- **Zero-Idle dilemma** — substantial economic loss and idle weighbridge resources caused by unpredictable farmer arrivals.
- **Opacity & inefficiency** — manual slot allocation prone to delays and lack of fair scheduling transparency.
- **The digital divide** — marginal farmers relying solely on basic feature phones are excluded from digital platforms.

## Proposed solution

- **Unified B2B platform** — smart SaaS ecosystem that automates APMC mandi weighbridge operations seamlessly.
- **Zero-Idle Trap Engine** — real-time dynamic rescheduling that plugs resource gaps instantly when a slot is missed.
- **Inclusive dual access** — Web PWA for smartphones + Toll-Free IVR Voice Assistant for feature phones.
- **End-to-end transparency** — automated digital token generation, instant SMS/OTP alerts, live administrative dashboards.

## Feasibility

- Multi-channel access, smart load-balancing scheduling, real-time Agmarknet sync, scalable MySQL 8.4 (Aiven).
- Operational: 4–8 h queues → scheduled digital gate passes; automatic no-show rescheduling; zero training via voice prompts.
- Business: B2B SaaS licensing across 7,000+ APMC mandis; optional per-transaction premium fees; minimal capex (a tablet/PC at the gate).
